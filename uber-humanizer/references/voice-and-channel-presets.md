# Voice and channel presets
These are adaptable defaults, not templates. Specific audience, brand, accessibility, and genre requirements win.

## Calibration
Use genuine samples or samples explicitly approved as voice references. Record observable diction, sentence rhythm, formality, humor, directness, and paragraph structure, with a short sample-supported observation for each. Separate stable voice from the mood of one piece. Do not copy incidental errors, personal facts, or distinctive phrases into unrelated work. Never infer biography, beliefs, expertise, or experience from style. AI drafts are provisional until approved.
Without samples, mark the internal voice profile provisional: direct, thoughtful, concrete, appropriately personable. Do not claim a proven match. For a calibration-only request, return the profile; otherwise use it quietly.

| Channel | Aim | Keep | Avoid imposing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Credible professional point with a reason | Relevant expertise and supplied examples | Mandatory hooks, line breaks after every sentence, engagement bait |
| Substack | Develop an editorial idea worth reading | Nuance, longer paragraphs, earned opinion | A mini-summary after every section |
| Reddit | Answer the actual discussion naturally | Useful brevity, uncertainty, genuine supplied experience | Invented product use, affiliations, testimonials, or consensus |
| Marketing | Connect a supported benefit to an audience need | Brand personality, persuasion, clear CTA | Neutralizing all sales language or inventing scarcity/results |
| Business | Make the request, decision, or update clear | Courtesy, exact schedules and commitments | Corporate filler or casualness that undermines the relationship |
| KDP nonfiction | Help the reader understand or do something | Definitions, examples, exercises, source qualifications | Unsupported expertise or a claim of KDP compliance from editing alone |
| Fiction | Serve scene, character, viewpoint, and tension | Fragments, distinct voices, subtext, unresolved emotion | Expository neutrality or identical character voices |
| Technical/educational | Enable understanding and accurate action | Stable terminology, headings, steps, useful repetition | Synonym variation that changes meaning or edits executable commands |

## Long work
Maintain three compact records: voice sheet; terminology list; continuity ledger. Mark section status as supplied, edited, or not reviewed. At assembly check chronology, facts, pronouns, repeated arguments, opening patterns, conclusions, and shifts in register. Preserve purposeful recurring motifs. If a document is incomplete, limit claims about review to the supplied sections.
