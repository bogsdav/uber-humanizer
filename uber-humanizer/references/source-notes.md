## Synthesis decisions
Editorial advice is a proposed choice about usefulness. Observed patterns describe tendencies and do not establish provenance. Empirical claims require inspectable methods and appropriate evidence; this package does not validate the supplied statistical claims.
Sources 7 and 10 explicitly reuse other guides; source 9 also cites overlapping material, and source 2 describes compilation. Similar lists are not independent confirmation. Do not infer a shared origin where it was not established.
Context resolves conflicts: exact quotations outrank punctuation preferences; evidence outranks decisiveness; supported specificity outranks vivid invented details; genre outranks blanket neutrality; logical structure outranks forced asymmetry; readable grammar outranks simulated imperfection. Personal warmth does not require autobiography. Marketing may persuade; fiction may invent; factual exposition must preserve uncertainty.
